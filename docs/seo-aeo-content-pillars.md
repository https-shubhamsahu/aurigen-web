## Publishing rules

1. Answer-first inverted pyramid on entity pages.
2. One clear H1; question-form H2s only when natural.
3. Name entities consistently: Aurigen, Thakur Shyamnarayan Engineering College, Robotics & Automation Club, Shubham Sahu, Neel Bhogle, Prof. Rita Vora.
4. No invented stats, awards, or backlink claims.
5. Prefer documentary photos from real labs over stock or AI imagery.
